Build an empire from the first fire to the information age.

Atlas of Ages is a turn-based 4X strategy game made for phones. Explore a living world, found cities, research, trade, worship and fight, and win by conquest, science, culture, faith or score.

Join the beta

A deep 4X that reads clearly

  • Eight nations with their own leaders, abilities, unique units and buildings.
  • Districts and adjacency, great people, religions, governments and policy cards.
  • Tactical combat with zones of control, flanking, fortification, siege and ships.
  • Rivals that plan, from Wayfarer to Ascendant: seven difficulty tiers; rivals reason better with every tier, and the top three also give them a head start.
  • A six-chapter tutorial that explains every rule with the game's real numbers.
  • Achievements and leaderboards on Google Play Games.

Free to try, one purchase for the whole game

Free: the tutorial, and endless games as the Kingdom of Solmere on a Small Continents map, on the first four difficulty tiers.

Full game, one purchase: seven more nations, Standard and Large maps, five more world types, the Imperator, Exalted and Ascendant tiers, and every future update.

No ads. No energy, no timers, nothing to grind.
