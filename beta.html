Join the Atlas of Ages beta

The game is in a closed test on Google Play before its public release. Testers play the newest build for free and help decide what it becomes. You need an Android phone or tablet.

  1. Join the testers group with the Google account you use on your phone: https://groups.google.com/g/atlas-of-ages-testers
  2. Open the test link and tap Become a tester: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.atlasofages.game
  3. Install from Google Play: after step 2 the test page links to the game in the Play Store. It can take a few minutes before the store shows it to you.
  4. Play, and tell us what you find. Tell us what you think on https://www.reddit.com/r/AtlasOfAges/ or by e-mail to support.AtlasOfAges@proton.me.

What helps most

  • Anything confusing in the tutorial or the first games.
  • Bugs, with what you did just before (a screenshot helps).
  • Balance: nations, difficulty tiers, victory types, rivals that feel unfair or too easy.
  • How the phone behaves: heat, battery, smoothness during rival turns.

Please stay in the test for at least two weeks: Google only lets the game go public after a long enough test.
